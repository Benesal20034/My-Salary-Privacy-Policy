1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Salary Calculator app handles user information. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

2. Information the app may process

  • Salary values and calculation inputs entered by the user.
  • Saved calculation results stored locally on the device when the user chooses to save them.
  • Basic technical and advertising-related data that may be collected by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

3. How information is used

  • To calculate salary-related values inside the app.
  • To store saved results locally on the device for the user's convenience.
  • To display ads and support the app through advertising services.

4. Local storage

Saved salary results are stored locally on the user's device. They are not intended to be publicly shared unless the user chooses to share them manually.

5. Advertising

The app may display ads using Google AdMob or similar advertising services. These services may collect device identifiers, advertising data, and other technical information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Third-party links and services

The app may include links to external pages such as privacy pages, app store pages, rating pages, or email/contact actions. Those services are governed by their own privacy practices.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers only to the extent necessary for app functionality, advertising, store services, or user-requested actions.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not specifically directed to children. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, please contact us and we will review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
